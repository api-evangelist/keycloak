FOSDEM is a free event for software developers to meet, share ideas and collaborate. Every year, thousands of developers of free and open source software from all over the world gather at the event. Several Keycloak related talks happen at FOSDEM in Brussels on January 31st and February 1st, and meet us and help out at the stand.
