‼️ Mark your calendars! Keycloak DevDay 2027 is coming back to Darmstadt, Germany , on April 8–9, 2027 ‼️ Like the previous edition, DevDay 2027 will span two days with the same overall structure – though with a few notable changes participants will notice: 📅 Thursday, April 8, 2027 – Conference Day : talks and deep dives from the Keycloak community and contributors. 📅 Friday, April 9, 2027 – Community Day : a more informal, hands-on day featuring a Hackathon and an OpenSpace with multiple breakout sessions, where attendees shape the agenda together.
