We are thrilled to announce that Keycloak will be at this year’s KubeCon NA, November 10-13th 2025, in Atlanta, Georgia. Keycloak’s presence at previous KubeCons was a huge success, and we are always eager to meet Keycloak enthusiasts, users and newcomers alike. At this year’s event we will be hosting a Kiosk in the Project Pavilion, as well as presenting multiple Keycloak talks.
