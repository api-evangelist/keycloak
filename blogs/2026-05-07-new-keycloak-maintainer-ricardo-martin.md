Ricardo joined the Keycloak IBM team at the beginning of 2023 and became one of the top Keycloak contributors since then. Ricardo has very deep knowledge in many core Keycloak areas. He is the most knowledgeable person from the whole team in areas like for example SAML, Passkeys or FIPS. His expertise is not limited to just those areas, but rather he has deep knowledge around pretty much every aspect of protocols and authentication in Keycloak.
