Highlights This release of Keycloak JS focuses on addressing several regressions that were introduced by accident. We apologize for any inconvenience these issues may have caused and thank our community for reporting them quickly and helping to verify the fixes. Bug Fixes Destructuring public methods now works correctly A regression was introduced that caused an error when destructuring public methods from a Keycloak instance.
