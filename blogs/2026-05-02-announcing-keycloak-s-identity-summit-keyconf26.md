Our annual conference dedicated to the Keycloak user community returns, with even more content and networking opportunities than last year. It’s the perfect place to interact, learn, share, and exchange insights and real-world use cases, network with fellow experts, users, and contributors. 📍 Introducing KEYCONF26 – taking place in Prague on October 8th, 2026! This year’s edition of the Keycloak Identity Summit features interactive sessions, and even more opportunities to engage with the people shaping the future of identity and access management.
