The Call for Papers for KeycloakCon Europe 2027 is now open! KeycloakCon Europe 2027 is a half-day, single-track conference in Barcelona on March 15, 2027 — co-located with KubeCon + CloudNativeCon Europe. After successful events in Amsterdam and Yokohama, KeycloakCon is back with talks, discussions, and community connections.
