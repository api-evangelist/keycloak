We are targeting Keycloak 26.6 to start supporting System for Cross-domain Identity Management (SCIM). The initial scope have been defined in this issue , but we want to ensure that we are addressing the most important use cases for our community. In order to better understand your needs and use cases around SCIM, we would greatly appreciate your participation in a brief survey.
