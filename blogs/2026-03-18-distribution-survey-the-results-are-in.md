<div class="paragraph">
<p>Earlier this year, we conducted a survey to better understand how the community deploys and manages Keycloak in the real world.</p>
</div>
<div class="paragraph">
<p>With over <strong>360 respondents</strong> (!!), the results provide a better understanding of the community&#8217;s needs and wishes.</p>
</div>
<div class="sect1">
<h2 id="_what_we_learned"><a class="anchor" href="#_what_we_learned"></a>What we learned</h2>
<div class="sectionbody">
<div class="paragraph">
<p>The survey results highlight a community that is deeply invested in the containerized ecosystem but is facing specific challenges in day-to-day operations.</p>
</div>
<div class="sect2">
<h3 id="_key_findings"><a class="anchor" href="#_key_findings"></a>Key Findings:</h3>
<div class="ulist">
<ul>
<li>
<p><strong>The Helm Charts:</strong> A significant portion of the community explicitly requested <strong>official Helm Charts</strong>, particularly for managing Keycloak installation and managing deployments via tools like ArgoCD.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Operator:</strong> Adoption of the Keycloak Operator is not very high in the community, which is mainly caused by the fact that the Operator setup is hard to grasp, and do not provide the required functionality.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>GitOps &amp; Configuration:</strong> There is a strong desire for more robust GitOps flows, especially when it comes to managing Keycloak resources like Realms and Clients.</p>
</li>
</ul>
</div>
<div class="paragraph">
<p>The whole Keycloak team is aware of these results and is discussing the next steps.</p>
</div>
<div class="paragraph">
<p><strong>Thank you to everyone who shared their feedback in this survey!</strong></p>
