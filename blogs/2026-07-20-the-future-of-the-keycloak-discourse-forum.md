The Keycloak project has always relied on open, searchable, community-owned discussion. Over the years, the Keycloak Discourse forum has been an important part of that: a place where users could ask configuration questions, share migration experiences, debug integrations, and find answers from people who had solved similar problems before. After a lot of consideration, we have decided to wind down the Keycloak Discourse forum.
